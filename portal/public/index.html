Round 1 — Explore

Ten questions whose answers are already in the files you just downloaded. Nothing to predict here — this round is about actually reading the data.

  • Expect joins and group-bys: totals per character across 28 films, comparisons between films, lookups across two files.
  • Answer each with a probability. Looked it up and certain? 100%. Didn't check? 50%.

60–90 minutes · 24 questions

Round 2 — Predict

Twenty-four questions about Phase 5, which hasn't happened. No file contains these answers. You have to model Phases 1–4 and extrapolate.

  • Scored by Brier score: an honest 60% beats a bluffed 95% that turns out wrong.
  • Skipping scores the same as answering 50% — a real shrug costs you nothing extra.
  • The dataset contains deliberate traps. Not every correlation you find is real.
Start Round 2

Round 3 — Draft

Claim one character you believe survives Phase 5. Exclusive: first team to claim a character takes them off the board for everyone else.

  • This is the only round with real time pressure — the pool shrinks all afternoon.
  • Your draft score posts at the end, when the organizers reveal Phase 5. It can't be scored per-team before then.

Round 4 — Report

Explore and Predict are graded automatically within seconds of each submission. Draft posts for everyone at once after the Phase 5 reveal. Report is judged by hand.
